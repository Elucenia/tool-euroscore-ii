## Source-specific finding

Royal Papworth terms restrict copies/derivatives of its website/calculator and assert trademark permission requirements for EuroSCORE names. Those service terms are not evidence that the published numerical model cannot be independently implemented. Brazilian law Art.132 IV separately describes noncommercial scientific/reference citation of marks; that is a limited context, not a commercial brand licence or international clearance.

EuroSCORE and EuroSCORE II are asserted by Royal Papworth Hospital as registered trademarks. The name is used here to identify the cited Nashef et al. 2012 scientific model. This is an independent ELUCENIA experimental implementation, is not the Royal Papworth calculator and has no claimed Royal Papworth endorsement or commercial trademark licence.
